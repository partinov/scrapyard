Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

Scrapyard: Random Defense collects nothing and sends nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. Your progress is a few files on your phone.

The long version

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier, and it contains no code that could send them anywhere: no networking, no analytics, no crash reporter and no advertising framework. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking, no collected data types and no accessed API types.

What is stored, and where

Three files, in the app's own container on your device:

FileWhat it holds
profile.jsonYour level, experience, skills, relic, runes, stage progress, recipe codex and settings
run.jsonThe run in progress, so closing the app does not lose it
runlog.jsonA local history of finished runs, capped at 300 records

These never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is backed up to a server operated by us, because there is no server operated by us.

The run log

The app keeps a local record of finished runs (waves cleared, units pulled, fusions made) so that you can export it yourself if you want to. Export only happens when you choose it, and the file goes wherever you send it. Until then, the log is just a file on your phone.

Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The app learns only whether Premium is owned on this device, never who bought it or how, and Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, for example if advertising is added, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the change will be visible in the app's App Store privacy label.
