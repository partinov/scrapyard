Scrapyard: Random Defense · iPhone

Common questions

Is there a limit on how many units I can hold?
No. The yard scrolls as it grows; tap the expand button to see all of it. To clear out the bottom of the table, use SELL JUNK in the expanded yard, which sells every Common and Uncommon, or BULK SELL to choose exactly which units go.
How do I fuse units?
Drag one card onto another to fuse a pair, or tap FUSE READY when it lights up. That makes every fusion you can complete, including recipes with three ingredients. Fusing consumes your weakest qualifying copies and produces one stronger unit. Each fusion multiplies that unit's damage by 1.6×, and the multiplier compounds. With Premium, fusions happen on their own.
What are the recipes?
Three lines, Fire, Arc and Heavy, each climbing one rarity at a time and meeting at Prime Mover, which takes one of each mythic. Every unit goes into exactly one recipe, and the Recipes screen shows where anything you hold goes next.
How do stages work?
Clear a stage — survive to wave 20 — to open the next one. There is no last stage. Every stage you clear for the first time makes your yard permanently a little stronger, so the stages behind you get easier while each new one asks a little more. Pick any stage you have opened from Main menu → Stage.
What do I get for killing a boss?
A boss arrives every tenth wave. Kill it before the clock runs out and it drops a free unit: Epic or better from the first boss, Legendary or better from the second on. A boss still standing when the clock runs out drops nothing and costs five lives.
What are the twists on some stages?
From stage 6, every third stage has a twist: Swarm (twice the enemies, half the health), Armoured (burn does half), Rush (a 26-second clock), Boss Rush (a boss every five waves) or Shielded (splash does half). The Stage screen shows a stage's twist before you play it.
What is today's run?
One run a day, the same for every player, at your record stage with the day's twist. You get one attempt — it is used when you start — and it pays double xp. Play on consecutive days to build a streak.
I have never seen some units. Can I get all of them?
Yes. Every unit can be pulled at its rarity, and most can also be built from the tier below; the Recipes screen labels each ingredient pull, build or pull or build. The rarest are the Epic and Legendary ones that are only pulled, such as Derrick Colossus and Gantry Crane; boss drops come from exactly those tables.
Why did my upgrade fail?
Upgrades can fail, and get less reliable the more a unit already has. The gas is spent either way. The Steady Hands skill improves the odds.
What is the difference between the pull tiers?
Price and floor. Scrap is cheap and mostly Common, Standard is the ordinary table, and Prime costs more and never rolls Common. Holding a pull button repeats it.
How do I get gas?
A lump every fifth wave, plus a bonus for a clean one, and the gas lottery, which is always open. Tap GAS to switch the pull row over to it.
What does Premium include?
A one-time purchase: fast-forward at 2× and 4×, auto-fuse, and auto-roll to a chosen rank. It cannot buy pulls, rerolls, ranks, gas or minerals. Luck in this game is never for sale.
I bought Premium on another device, or reinstalled the game.
Main menu → Settings → Premium → RESTORE PURCHASES.
Are there ads?
No.
Can I move my progress to another device?
Not yet. Progress is stored on the device only.

Reporting a problem

keksaingress@gmail.com

Please include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your level and the wave you were on. Every run is driven by a seeded simulation, so that is usually enough to reproduce it.

Accessibility

The game supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion. If something is unreadable or not announced, please report it as a bug.
